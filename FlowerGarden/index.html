Welcome to

Interactive Garden

Plant a garden with your mouse or finger, then watch it live through the day and night.

  1. 1
    Click or tap anywhere to plant. The higher you click, the taller the flower grows.
  2. 2
    Pick a flower at the bottom: daisy, tulip, sunflower, rose, lavender, or a surprise mix.
  3. 3
    Water a flower to help it grow, or make it rain on the whole garden.
  4. 4
    Watch the sky: the sun sets, stars and fireflies come out, and butterflies and bees visit by day.
  5. 5
    Keep it: your garden saves itself. Save a picture or copy a link to share it.
